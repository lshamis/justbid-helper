🪄 JustBid Helper Setup

This page helps you set up a tool that automatically highlights good items on JustBid.com. It will put a bright green border around items that are "Appears New", and mark others in red.

Step 1: Install the Helper Extension

First, you must add a helper to your web browser. Click the button below that matches the browser you use:

Click Here for Google Chrome (or Brave)

Click Here for Firefox

Note: When the page opens, click the blue button that says "Add to Chrome" or "Add to Firefox", then click "Add extension" on the popup alert.

Step 2: Add the JustBid Helper Script

After you finish Step 1, click the green button below:

Click Here to Install the JustBid Helper Script

Note: A new screen will open. Look for a button that says "Confirm Installation" (usually near the top or left) and click it. Once it says "Installed", you can close that tab.

Step 3: Go Shopping!

You are done! Now open a new tab and go to www.justbid.com. The helper will work automatically in the background.
